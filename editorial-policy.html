Editorial Policy

At NutriRoute, we are committed to providing our users with accurate, objective, and reliable nutrition data. This Editorial Policy explains our standards for sourcing, updating, and reviewing the nutritional information published across our calculators.

1. Data Accuracy and Sourcing

All nutrition data (calories, protein, fat, carbohydrates) published on NutriRoute is compiled from official, publicly released manufacturer data, brand menus, or verified nutritional databases. We do not rely on user-submitted or unverified estimations.

2. Review and Verification

Every brand calculator is built and manually double-checked by our editorial team. In cases where options and customizations are dynamic (e.g., custom milk or size swaps), we verify that calculations align precisely with the offset values published by the brand.

3. Regular Content Updates

Menus and ingredients change frequently. Our team routinely monitors restaurant chains for menu updates, limited-time offers, and ingredient modifications to keep our calculations up to date.

4. Editorial Independence

NutriRoute is an independent educational platform. We do not accept funding or sponsorship from restaurant brands to bias or highlight specific products. All recommendations and calculations are driven purely by data.
