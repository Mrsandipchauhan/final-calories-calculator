About NutriRoute

NutriRoute is an independent digital resource dedicated to bringing transparency, clarity, and precision to food nutrition and calorie tracking.

Established in 2026, NutriRoute was founded with a simple objective: to eliminate the guesswork from daily nutrition. While many platforms force users to log foods in complex diaries or pay for premium features, we believe that understanding what you eat should be straightforward, free, and accessible to everyone.

Our Sourcing & Methodology

We compile data with care. Every nutritional profile published on NutriRoute is derived from official, manufacturer-published documents, USDA databases, and verified product specifications. We do not support user-submitted metrics to prevent errors and ensure that our estimates remain as reliable as possible.

For custom orders (like selecting alternative milks, size modifiers, or topping removals), our calculator dynamically applies offset calculations based on the brand's specifications, giving you a custom estimate rather than just a baseline guess.

Editorial Independence

NutriRoute is entirely self-funded and independent. We have no brand relationships, sponsorships, or corporate partnerships with the dining chains or food brands listed on our platform. All data is evaluated objectively. Our primary goal is to empower consumers with accurate statistics, allowing them to balance their diets without bias.

Our Expert Editorial Team

Our research, database updates, and calculations are monitored by certified dietitians and nutrition advocates. You can read more about their academic backgrounds, credentials, and responsibilities on our official Editorial Team Page.
