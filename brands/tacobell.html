Making Healthy Choices at Taco Bell

You can easily build a low-calorie, high-protein meal at Taco Bell by following a few simple rules:

The "Fresco Style" Hack

Ask for any item "Fresco Style." This replaces the cheese, sour cream, and mayo-based sauces with freshly diced tomatoes (pico de gallo). It instantly saves you about 50 calories and 5g of fat per item without sacrificing flavor.

Swap to Chicken

Swapping the standard seasoned beef for grilled chicken reduces fat and calories while maintaining or slightly increasing the protein content. A chicken soft taco is much leaner than a beef soft taco.

Focus on the Tacos

Burrito tortillas are large and calorie-dense (up to 200 calories just for the flour tortilla). Ordering 3 crunchy tacos instead of a large burrito gives you more protein and more filling food for fewer empty carbs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the lowest-calorie item at Taco Bell?

A standard Crunchy Taco (Beef) is only 170 calories. If you order it Fresco style, it drops to roughly 140 calories, making it an excellent low-calorie option if you want to eat several items.

Is the Bean Burrito healthy?

Yes, the Bean Burrito is an excellent source of vegetarian protein (13g) and dietary fiber (9g) for only 350 calories. It is widely considered one of the healthiest fast-food options available anywhere.

Are Taco Bell's Power Bowls good for macros?

Yes, the Chicken Power Menu Bowl contains 460 calories and an excellent 26g of protein. It is highly customizable and perfect for people tracking their macros.

Does Taco Bell have keto options?

Keto at Taco Bell is difficult because of the shells and tortillas. Your best option is ordering a Power Menu Bowl and asking for no rice and no beans, replacing them with extra meat, cheese, and guacamole.

Are the sauces at Taco Bell high in calories?

The standard border sauces (Mild, Hot, Fire, Diablo) have effectively zero calories. However, the creamy sauces (like Spicy Ranch, Creamy Jalapeno, or Chipotle sauce) add roughly 30-50 calories per serving.

What does "Supreme" mean?

Ordering an item "Supreme" generally means adding sour cream and diced tomatoes. This typically adds about 30 to 40 calories (mostly fat from the sour cream) to the standard item.

Can I track macros accurately here?

Our estimates are based on Taco Bell's standardized portioning tools (the specific scoops used for beef, beans, and cheese). However, variations happen during busy rushes, so consider these numbers highly accurate estimates.
