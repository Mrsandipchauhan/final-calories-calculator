Subway Nutrition & Macro Tracking Guide

Subway has built its brand on being the healthy fast-food alternative, and for good reason. With an incredibly modular menu, it is one of the easiest places to hit your daily protein goals without blowing past your calorie limits. Using our independent Subway calorie calculator, you can track exactly how every slice of cheese or dollop of mayo impacts your sandwich. And if you are eating on the go, check out our Panera Bread Calorie Calculator or our Starbucks Nutrition Calculator to keep your macros dialed in all day long.

How to Use the Subway Calorie Calculator

Because every sandwich is custom-built, tracking can feel overwhelming. Here is how to use our tool effectively:

  • Start with the Base: Select your core sub (e.g., Turkey Breast, Sweet Onion Teriyaki, or Italian B.M.T.).
  • Adjust the Size: Toggle between a 6-inch and a Footlong. Keep in mind that a Footlong literally doubles all macros (calories, carbs, and sodium).
  • Customize the Bread & Cheese: This is where the hidden calories lie. See how switching from Italian Herbs & Cheese to 9-Grain Wheat changes your macros.

Making Healthy Choices at Subway

It is incredibly easy to build a low-calorie, high-protein meal at Subway if you know what to ask for:

The "Protein Bowl" Hack

If you are watching your carbs, you can order any sub as a "Protein Bowl" (a salad). Dropping the 6-inch bread immediately removes about 200 calories and nearly 40g of carbohydrates from your meal.

Sauce Strategy

Mayonnaise and Ranch are the primary reasons healthy subs become calorie bombs. A standard squiggle of Mayo adds roughly 100 calories and 11g of fat. Swap it for Mustard (5 calories), Vinegar, or Sweet Onion Sauce to save massive amounts of fat.

Double Protein, Not Double Cheese

If you are trying to hit high protein goals (e.g., 40g+ per meal), always choose "Double Meat" rather than trying to get protein from cheese. Adding provolone only adds 4g of protein but comes with 50 calories of pure fat.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the lowest-calorie sandwich at Subway?

The 6-inch Veggie Delite on 9-Grain Wheat (no cheese, no mayo) is the lowest calorie sub at just 200 calories. For meat options, the 6-inch Turkey Breast is only 260 calories.

Is the Subway Tuna healthy?

While tuna itself is healthy, Subway's tuna salad is heavily mixed with mayonnaise. A 6-inch Tuna sub contains about 450 calories and 25g of fat, making it one of the most calorie-dense "healthy" options on the menu.

Which Subway bread has the lowest calories?

The 9-Grain Wheat and the Artisan Italian (White) are typically the lowest calorie options, hovering around 190-200 calories for a 6-inch portion.

Does Subway have Keto options?

Yes! By ordering a "Protein Bowl" (which removes the bread) and sticking to non-glazed meats (like turkey, chicken strips, or cold cuts) and vinegar/oil dressings, you can easily create a keto-friendly meal under 10g of net carbs.

Are the cookies at Subway high in calories?

Yes, a standard Subway cookie (like Chocolate Chip or Macadamia Nut) contains around 200 to 220 calories. Eating two cookies adds over 400 empty calories to your meal.

Does ordering a wrap save calories over bread?

Surprisingly, no. Subway's wraps are large and dense. A standard wrap actually contains around 290 calories and 50g of carbs, which is more than a standard 6-inch sub roll.

Can I track macros accurately here?

Subway uses standardized scoops and pre-portioned meat slices, making base macro tracking highly accurate. However, the amount of sauce or extra veggies the "Sandwich Artist" applies can alter the final numbers slightly.
