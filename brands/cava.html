Making Healthy Choices at CAVA

CAVA's menu is naturally rich in wholesome ingredients, but calories can quickly stack up with multiple dips and oils. Keep these tips in mind:

Base Smartly

A full Grain Bowl base contains 280 calories. Swapping to a Greens + Grains base brings it down to 200 calories, while a pure Salad Bowl base is only 45 calories. Mixing supergreens with your grains is a great way to bulk up your volume for fewer calories.

Watch the Creamy Spreads

Spreads like Crazy Feta (70 cal) and Hummus (70 cal) are delicious but add extra fats. Swapping one of them for Tzatziki (30 cal) or Harissa (45 cal) preserves taste while reducing calorie density.

Drizzle Wisely

CAVA's Garlic Dressing adds 130 calories per serving. The Lemon Herb Tahini (110 cal) and Greek Vinaigrette (140 cal) are also calorie-dense. Opting for a light drizzle, or choosing Yogurt Dill (40 cal), keeps your meal clean and light.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the lowest calorie base at CAVA?

The Salad Bowl base is the lowest calorie option at just 45 calories. It features fresh greens like romaine, spinach, and supergreens.

Are CAVA's dips and spreads healthy?

Yes, they are made with fresh ingredients like chickpeas, sesame paste (tahini), and yogurt. However, they are calorie-dense, so moderate your portions if tracking macros.

How much protein is in CAVA's Grilled Chicken?

A standard serving of Grilled Chicken provides 26g of high-quality protein for only 170 calories and 6g of fat, making it an excellent macro-friendly choice.

Is CAVA suitable for a keto diet?

Absolutely. If you choose a Salad Bowl base, add Grilled Chicken or Steak, select dips like Tzatziki or Crazy Feta, and choose a low-carb dressing like Yogurt Dill, you get a premium high-protein, low-carb keto meal.
