How to Use the Qdoba Nutrition Calculator

Tracking your bowl shouldn't be complicated. Here is how to get the most accurate nutrition estimate for your next Qdoba run:

  • Choose Your Base: Bowl or Salad (0-15 calories), Burrito (~300 cal), Tacos or Quesadilla. The base alone can be the difference between 650 and 950 calories.
  • Add Your Protein: Grilled Adobo Chicken (~190 cal) is leanest. Grilled Steak or Impossible Plant-Based are also great options.
  • Build with Toppings: Add Rice, Beans, Pico de Gallo, and Fajita Veggies. Watch out for Cheese, Sour Cream, and Guacamole where fat climbs fastest.

Making Healthy Choices at Qdoba

Bowl instead of Burrito

Skips the tortilla entirely. Saves ~300 calories.

Fajita Veggies or Beans instead of Rice

Adds fiber while trimming calories. Saves 170-190 calories.

Pico de Gallo or Salsa instead of Cheese & Sour Cream

Flavor with almost no sodium. Saves 100-150 calories.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How many calories are in a Qdoba-style bowl?

A standard Grilled Chicken Bowl with rice, black beans, pico, and cheese runs around 650 calories with 47g of protein.

How many calories are in a Qdoba-style burrito?

A loaded Chicken Burrito with rice, beans, pico, and cheese runs about 950 calories.

What's the healthiest order?

A Grilled Chicken Salad with black beans, pico de gallo, and guacamole, no dressing, no cheese, comes in around 435 calories with 39g of protein and 19g of fiber.

Can I build a keto or low-carb order?

Yes, a Steak Keto Bowl runs around 385 calories with roughly 14g of total carbs.
