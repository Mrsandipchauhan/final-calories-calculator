Nutritional Calculation Methodology

To keep macro tracking simple and useful, NutriRoute employs a structured calculation methodology for determining dynamic food and meal profiles.

1. Baseline Nutrition Sourcing

For every item, we establish a baseline calculation using the standard recipe as specified by the manufacturer. This baseline reflects the default size, standard ingredients, and default preparation style.

2. Dynamic Offsets for Customizations

When you swap or customize ingredients, our calculator dynamically applies offset metrics. For example, if you replace whole milk (default) with almond milk, we subtract the whole milk values and add the almond milk values for that specific portion size.

3. Transparency and Rounding

We present nutrition figures rounded to the nearest whole unit (calories, grams of macros) to remain consistent with standard FDA labeling guidelines used by brands.
